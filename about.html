Our story

A Kaduna institution, built on trust, craft, and care.

A.S.D Motors Nigeria Limited has served Kaduna and Northern Nigeria as an authorized Peugeot dealer for more than two decades — selling brand-new automobiles and running a fully equipped service garage staffed by Peugeot-certified professionals in both Kaduna and Abuja.

Our reputation was not built on advertising. It was built one customer at a time — by showing up, by doing the work properly, and by treating every client as though they were our first.
